System Architecture & Research Blueprint

Research, architecture, and implementation blueprint for TC-Fusion

Developed by: Team Cyber Titans

The Operational Bottlenecks

  • 1. Asynchronous Observations: Polar-orbiting microwave and geostationary IR imagers have time offsets from 15 min to hours.
  • 2. Center Estimation Ambiguity: High cirrus canopies obscure low-level circulation centers, introducing center fix errors.
  • 3. Rapid Intensification Blind Spots: Traditional numerical models miss sudden convective surges (≥30kt/24h).
  • 4. Subsurface Ocean Decoupling: SST alone fails because cold wake upwelling limits intensification unless backed by high Ocean Heat Content (OHC).

TC-FUSION's Multimodal Innovation

  • 1. Lagrangian Spatio-Temporal Alignment: Translates asynchronous sensor swaths along the cyclone motion vector to a common reference epoch (t₀).
  • 2. Cross-Attention Deep Fusion: Uses dual-backbone Vision Transformers to fuse microwave inner-core precipitation with thermal cloud symmetry.
  • 3. Thermodynamic Ocean Heat Engine: Incorporates altimetry-derived OHC and satellite SST for physics-informed potential intensity.
  • 4. Transparent RI Diagnostics: Physics-based factor breakdown for disaster management and coastal evacuation planning.
